# 1. 課題/Theme

6人の住むシェアハウス

Shared House for 6 people 

# 2. 課題の主旨/ Design Brief

東京都内の住宅街に建つ、6人の住むシェアハウスを設計する。シェアハウスは、リビングルーム・ダイニングルーム・厨房及び水回り等を共用する賃貸住宅で、住人はそれぞれ個室を持つ。6人は20～30歳程度の学生や会社員を想定し、各人のプライバシーや生活時間帯の違いに配慮しながら、良好な共同生活を送ることができる施設とする。また、1階にはオーナーが運営する50m²程度のカフェ（厨房を含む）を設け、居住者だけでなく近隣の住民も集まれるような場所とする。

Design Shared House to be shared by six people in a residential area in Tokyo. Shared house is a rental apartment with a common area(s) including living room, dining room, kitchen, bathroom and in addition six private rooms for the residents. The house accommodates young students and office workers age between twenty and thirty years old. The design of the house should be considered privacy concerns of the residents who may have a different daily schedule from each other. The house provides a café equipped with a kitchen on the ground floor run by the owner of the house. It is approx. 50m², and it is used as a communal place by the residents as well as by its neighbors. 

# 3. 敷地と周辺環境 / Site and Context

The site is a flat and rectangular shape (20m frontage x 18m depth) as shown in Fig.1. A public road width 6m is adjacent to south side of the site. A train station is located west of the street reachable by foot in 5 minutes. Lot Coverage is 80% and Floor Area Ratio is 100%. The height limit is 10m above the ground.
